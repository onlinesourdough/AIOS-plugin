# Ask, wait and resume

Use for Interview and for Setup's individual questions; reading this procedure
does not activate a full interview. Ask one material question in the user's
language. Keep open questions open; optional examples must not restrict replies.

Use an available native question tool only when its actual runtime/mode rules
permit that question. An asynchronous tool may be usable while another is
Plan-only. Do not replace a usable tool with a prose-only question. If none is
usable or the call fails, briefly explain and ask in conversation. Never invent
restrictive choices just to fit a tool. Reuse ordinary-message answers too.

Silence or a preselected option is not an answer or consent. Required input
waits while independent authorized work continues. Optional unanswered questions
follow the runtime's rules; waiting longer supplies no missing authority.
Do not repeat a pending question or pretend it has been answered.

For a required asynchronous answer in an active interview or setup, keep the
turn open rather than sending a final handback. After useful independent work,
use permitted interruptible waits and check for new input between them. A timeout
does not answer the question. Honor an explicit skip, pause, cancellation or
change of task immediately. The runtime's higher-priority wait and turn rules
govern; disclose a genuine limit instead of promising that a form survives
completion.

On interruption, retain the subject, accepted answers, source pointers, pending
question and next action in the task's existing evidence when needed. Resume
from that point, rechecking only stale or affected facts. Setup additionally
retains its actual changes, readback and off choices. Do not restart discovery
or treat method feedback as a fact about the user's business.
