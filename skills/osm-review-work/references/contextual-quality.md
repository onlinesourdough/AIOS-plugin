# Contextual quality in Review

Select only checks relevant to the actual deliverable. Freeze the subject and
name the applicable client context, brief, reference corpus or brand source.
Accepted conversation decisions are also valid sources. Retrieve only scoped,
authorized material. Do not import the method author's personal preferences or
invent a client's missing values. If a missing source materially prevents the
judgment, report that gap; otherwise assess the supported generic checks and
label the unverified part. Do not require a voice audit for unrelated code.

## Principles — a material plan, recommendation or completed approach

Use the client's sourced principles to interpret tradeoffs. Check that the
result addresses the actual constraint; impact justifies effort; owner work is
minimized; material claims are verified; useful existing assets are reused;
and autonomy stays within the accepted authority and stop conditions. These
six observations do not supply a client's goals, preferred strategy or values.
State evidence and a material correction when a tradeoff fails; do not turn a
preference into a universal rule or add a ceremonial score.

## Voice — owner-facing writing

Check the accepted language and mode; style fit with the selected reference
corpus; sentence length where that corpus or task supplies a preference;
traceability of factual claims; and grounded, precise language. These five checks have no
built-in house style, punctuation ban, fixed sentence length or mandatory
persona. Preserve the client's chosen voice. A missing corpus is not license to
claim a verified style match; use explicit task direction and label the limit.

Use the bundled [human-writing](../../osm-human-writing/SKILL.md) method for
substantive prose drafting or revision. Preserve accepted facts, uncertainty and
voice; the method adds no separate acceptance gate. Ordinary code review does
not need a writing pass.

## Visual — a generated or edited visual artifact

Open the actual final render, not only a generation prompt or source document.
Check text correctness; fit with the brief and available brand reference;
legibility at the intended viewing size; required facts and elements; and
requested dimensions and export format. These five checks need observed output.
When the final render is unavailable, that part is NOT VERIFIED. Correct a
material defect in Build, render again and review the changed output.

## Return into the same Review

Record applicable checks, observed evidence, material findings and unverified
parts under the same PASS, REVISE or BLOCKED gate. No subject edits during the
pass. Recheck only affected judgments after a change, then perform final
completeness and publish safety at their own checkpoints when applicable.
